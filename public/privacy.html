Privacy at GroupNote

Draft policy — review and replace your contact details before public launch.

GroupNote stores the card information and messages you submit in its database to make group cards work. Anyone with a card's invitation link can read the messages and submit a new one while the card is open. Treat invitation links as shared access, not strict privacy.

We do not ask for accounts, addresses, or payment information. Submitted names, occasions and message text are stored until deleted by the site operator. The card organiser can permanently delete the entire card and its messages using their private organiser link. If you contributed a message and want it removed, contact the organiser who invited you. A support contact must be supplied by the site operator before a public launch.

For basic abuse prevention we may store daily salted hashes derived from network addresses and counts of requests. The site also saves organiser keys locally in your browser so you can return to a card.

The hosting provider may process technical connection information as needed to deliver the service. Do not place confidential, highly sensitive, or private information in group cards.

This site currently does not use advertising or analytics cookies. If these features are added, this notice will need to be updated.
