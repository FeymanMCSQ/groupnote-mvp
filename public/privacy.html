Privacy at GroupNote

Last updated: 3 November 2026

GroupNote lets organisers create group greeting cards and contributors add messages without accounts. This notice explains how the current free version handles information.

What we collect and why

We store the details you submit when creating or signing a card: the occasion, card title, recipient and organiser names, introductory note, selected theme, contributor names, messages and emoji. We also store creation times and whether contributions are open or closed. We use this information to display cards, collect messages and let organisers manage their cards.

The app does not require accounts, email addresses, phone numbers or payment details to create or sign a card. Any personal information you choose to put in a name or message becomes part of that card.

For basic abuse prevention, we store daily salted hashes derived from network addresses, dates, action types and request counts. The app uses these records to limit card creation and message submissions.

Who can see a card

Anyone with a card's invitation link can read its contents and add messages while contributions are open. A link can be forwarded by anyone who has it. Closing contributions stops new messages; it does not stop people with the link from reading existing messages.

The private organiser link allows its holder to close, reopen or delete the card. Keep that link private and use Copy invite link when inviting contributors. Avoid putting confidential or sensitive information in cards.

Storage and other services

The app saves an organiser key in the browser's local storage when a card is created. Deleting the card through its organiser link removes that saved key from the browser used for deletion. Copies saved in other browsers are not automatically cleared.

The current app does not include advertising or analytics tracking. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which requires your browser to connect to Google. When GroupNote is hosted online, the hosting provider also processes technical connection information needed to deliver the service. The app is configured to use Cloudflare for hosting and database storage.

How long information stays

Cards and their messages have no automatic expiry. They remain in the app's database until the organiser or site operator deletes them. Deleting a card removes its associated messages from the app's database. Closing a card does not delete its data.

Abuse-prevention records currently have no automatic deletion schedule. Deleting a card does not delete those separate records. Deleting a card also cannot remove copies that other people have saved, printed or exported.

Questions, corrections and removal requests

To ask about your information, request a correction or removal, or raise a privacy concern, contact the GroupNote operator using the details below. Contributors can also contact the organiser who invited them. There is currently no self-service option to edit or delete a single message.

Include the card's invitation link and enough information to identify your contribution. Do not send the private organiser key or unrelated sensitive information. If you contact us by email or phone, we will receive the contact details and information you choose to share so we can respond to your request.
